# REVO Privacy Notice

**Effective July 19, 2026**

REVO is a local Windows capture application. The current release has no account system, advertising, analytics, telemetry, crash-report uploads, cloud storage, or automatic media uploads.

## Data REVO Handles

REVO processes the display or game/window source you select, Windows desktop audio, and—only when enabled—the microphone you select. This data is processed locally on your PC to maintain a replay buffer and create clips or recordings.

REVO stores the following data locally:

- Clips: `%USERPROFILE%\Videos\REVO\Clips`
- Recordings: `%USERPROFILE%\Videos\REVO\Recordings`
- Settings: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\REVO\settings.ini`
- Diagnostic logs: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\REVO\revo.log`

Diagnostic logs may contain application status, hardware and encoder names, selected capture source labels, and error details. They do not intentionally contain recorded video or audio.

## Sharing and Retention

REVO does not transmit these files to its developers or any third party. Media and diagnostic logs remain on your device until you delete them.

Uninstalling REVO does not automatically delete clips, recordings, settings, or logs.

If you choose to share a clip, recording, or diagnostic log, the destination's terms and privacy practices apply. Review logs before sharing, as window and capture source labels may reveal personal information.

## Changes and Contact

If a future REVO release introduces network services or materially changes how data is handled, this notice will be updated before those changes are released.

For questions or privacy-related concerns, use the project's official GitHub Issues page.
